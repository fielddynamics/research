## 4. Component 2 — torsion / contorsion curvature

R^T(\Gamma) = R(\{\} + K) - R(\{\}) \sim \nabla K + K^2.

Define the local TetA/TetB overlap field

$$

$$

The coefficient $\mathcal{K}_P$ is **not free** — it's controlled by the parity impedance

At $K=4$: $Z_4 = 17/13$.

## 10. Projection back to GR

The GR projector kills or hides each non-LC sector:

$$
\Pi_{\rm torsion=0} G_{\mu\nu}^{T} = 0,
$$
$$
\Pi_{\rm even/local} G_{\mu\nu}^{P} \to \text{averaged stress-energy contribution},
$$
$$
\Pi_{\rm local} G_{\mu\nu}^{J} \to \text{renormalized coupling } G,
$$
$$
\Pi_{\rm avg} G_{\mu\nu}^{R} \to T_{\mu\nu},
$$
$$
\Pi_{\rm local} G_{\mu\nu}^{M} = 0.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\;
\Pi_{\rm GR} \mathbb{G}_{\mu\nu}^{AB} = G_{\mu\nu}^{\rm LC},
\qquad
\Pi_{\rm GR} \mathbb{T}_{\mu\nu}^{AB} = T_{\mu\nu}.
\;}
$$

The Einstein equation is the quotient

$$
\boxed{\;
G_{\mu\nu}^{\rm LC} = \frac{8\pi G}{c^4} T_{\mu\nu}.

$$

In the full framework $G$ itself has a sector origin:

$$
\boxed{\;
G = \alpha^{21} \cdot \frac{17}{13} \cdot \frac{g_e}{2} \cdot \frac{\hbar c}{m_e^2}.
\;}
$$

**The 17/13 in this formula is the parity-overlap coefficient from sector P; the $\alpha^{21}$ and $g_e/2$ are the hinge coefficient $\lambda_e$ from sector J.** GR inherits the value of Newton's constant from sectors it cannot see.
