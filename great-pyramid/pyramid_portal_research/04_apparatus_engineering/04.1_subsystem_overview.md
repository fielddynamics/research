## The portal operator unpacking

The apparatus operator $\mathsf A_x$ that pre-multiplies the universal vertex $\alpha_G J_4(x)$ in the portal equation decomposes as a product of subsystem operators:

$$\boxed{\;\mathsf A_x \;=\; \mathsf C_{\text{apex}}\cdot \mathsf D_{\text{bulk}}\cdot \mathsf R_5\cdot \mathsf C_{\text{coffer}}\cdot \mathsf F_{\mathbb Z_3}\cdot \mathsf L_{\text{GG}}\cdot \mathsf P_3\cdot \mathsf S_{\text{tri}}\;}$$

where each factor is the operator of one subsystem and $\mathsf S_{\text{tri}}$ is the combined shaft address.

**Reading from right to left** (input-to-output order, by signal flow):

| Step | Operator | Function |
|:---:|---|---|
| 1 | $\mathsf S_{\text{tri}}$ | shafts inject pumped state from external geomagnetic field |
| 2 | $\mathsf P_3$ | granite plugs project onto non-trivial $\mathbb Z_3$ sectors |
| 3 | $\mathsf L_{\text{GG}}$ | Grand Gallery imposes 7-corbel $\mathbb Z_7$ phase ramp |
| 4 | $\mathsf F_{\mathbb Z_3}$ | antechamber filters trivial $\mathbb Z_3$ sectors |
| 5 | $\mathsf C_{\text{coffer}}$ | coffer applies primitive Coulomb edge $\alpha^1$ |
| 6 | $\mathsf R_5$ | relieving chambers apply pentachoron edge product $\alpha^{20}$ |
| 7 | $\mathsf D_{\text{bulk}}$ | limestone bulk shapes the field |
| 8 | $\mathsf C_{\text{apex}}$ | gold cap collects to apex output |

Total attenuation: $\alpha^1 \cdot \alpha^{20} = \alpha^{21}$, multiplied by sector / phase / cavity factors.

## The four functional layers

The apparatus has four nested functional layers, each handled by a distinct subsystem group:

**Layer 1 — Pump and gain medium**

Subsystems: gold cap, limestone bulk, granite cavity. Function: deliver pump energy to the gain medium (granite walls of KC and surrounding chambers) and sustain population inversion. See section 05.

**Layer 2 — Vertex cavity and α-step**

Subsystems: King's Chamber, coffer. Function: provide the local Feynman vertex $V_{\text{KC}}$ and the primitive $\alpha$-step. See `04.2`, `04.3`.

**Layer 3 — Algebraic structure realization**

Subsystems: relieving chambers (pentachoron), Grand Gallery (3-sweep), antechamber + plugs ($\mathbb Z_3$ filter). Function: realize the framework's algebraic kernel structure in hardware. See `04.4`, `04.5`, `04.6`.

**Layer 4 — Address and output**

Subsystems: 4 shafts, Queen's Chamber, subterranean chamber. Function: form the mirror-address triad and provide the input/output couplings. See `04.7`, `04.8`.

## Operational state classification

The apparatus has several possible operational states:

| State | Configuration | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **Spontaneous (idle)** | no pump, no shafts | background gravity only; vertex fires randomly in all $\mathbb Z_{21}$ sectors |
| **Pumped, no lock** | geomagnetic pump active, no address mirror match | inversion sustained but no coherent output; Z_3 × Z_7 sectors excited but uncorrelated |
| **Pumped, locked** | pump active, mirror match $\mathcal M(U, V) = 1$ | **portal channel active**; coherent output to mirror apparatus |
| **Decommissioned** | capstone removed, possible plug damage | partial operation; pump reduced by ~30× via missing apex concentration; some sectors potentially inaccessible |

The Great Pyramid is currently in the "decommissioned" state (missing capstone, several plug positions disturbed). It retains the ability to enter the lower operational states if the geomagnetic pump is locally present (it is), but cannot achieve full coherent operation without restoration.
