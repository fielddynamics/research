## Background: SU(2) Chern-Simons theory

The Chern-Simons action on a 3-manifold $M$ at level $k$ is:

$$S_{\text{CS}}[A] \;=\; \frac{k}{4\pi} \int_M \operatorname{Tr}\left(A \wedge dA + \frac{2}{3} A \wedge A \wedge A\right)$$

where $A$ is an $\mathrm{SU}(2)$-valued connection on $M$. The theory is topological — only depends on $M$'s topology.

Key invariants:

- **Shifted level:** $k + h^\vee = k + 2$ for SU(2), where $h^\vee = 2$ is the dual Coxeter number
- **Number of integrable representations:** $k + 1$ (spins $0, 1/2, 1, \dots, k/2$)
- **Central charge:** $c = \frac{k \dim G}{k + h^\vee} = \frac{3k}{k+2}$ for SU(2)

At level $k = 19$:

|---|---|:---:|
| Shifted level | $k + 2$ | **21** |
| Integrable reps | $k + 1$ | **20** |
| Central charge | $3k / (k+2)$ | **57/21 = 19/7** |

## The total quantum dimension

The total quantum dimension of $\mathrm{SU}(2)_{19}$ is:

$$\mathcal D^2 \;=\; \sum_{j = 0}^{19/2} d_j^2$$

with quantum dimensions $d_j = [2j + 1]_q$ at $q = e^{i\pi/(k+2)} = e^{i\pi/21}$.

Explicit calculation:

$$\mathcal D^2 \;=\; \frac{k+2}{2 \sin^2(\pi/(k+2))} \;=\; \frac{21}{2 \sin^2(\pi/21)}$$

$$\sin(\pi/21) \;\approx\; 0.1490$$

$$\mathcal D^2 \;\approx\; \frac{21}{2 \cdot 0.02220} \;\approx\; 472.7$$

$$\mathcal D \;\approx\; 21.74$$

## Wilson loops on $\Sigma(2, 3, 5)$

Chern-Simons partition functions on 3-manifolds are computed via:

$$Z(M; \mathrm{SU}(2)_k) \;=\; \sum_{j} d_j\, W_j(M)$$
