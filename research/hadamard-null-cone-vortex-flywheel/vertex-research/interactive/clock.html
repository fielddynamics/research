What the clock surface does not prove

The co-rotating Killing clock norm is generally not the hypersurface-normal ADM lapse. The boundary's clock rate is constant, but its normal gravitational gradient remains nonzero away from the two saddle points. Closure therefore does not derive a zero TetB response without a scale-sensitive physical selection law.
