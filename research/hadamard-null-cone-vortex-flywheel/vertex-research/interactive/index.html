Interactive research atlas

Six offline explorers for the cumulative scheme, antenna, spectral and constitutive studies. The controls change the actual calculations or replay the recorded Python trajectories.

01 / Root separation and recursive time face

Rotate the relative registration; inspect the cross-Gram and compare complete subdivision with the Sierpiński gasket.

02 / Registered 16-channel interaction

Adjust d, a and k; examine native and Walsh matrices, rank, determinant and hierarchy mixing.

03 / Collective circulation

Scrub or replay the recorded Stage VII and nonlinear witness trajectories.

04 / Constitutive saturation and stability

Explore μ, dual curvature, threshold, gain, seeded flux and the recorded perturbation trajectories.

05 / Ordered response and drain

Compare capacity and parallel boundary closures, and calculate exact native coherent exchange.

06 / Aperture and seed recruitment

Sharp distributed-capacity bounds, exact seed-instability threshold, and nonlinear trajectory replay.

How to use

Open any HTML file in a modern browser after extracting the ZIP. All JavaScript, styles and trajectory samples are embedded; no internet or server is required. Numerical plots are from the existing CSVs, not new simulations. Sliders marked analytic evaluate formulas in real time.
