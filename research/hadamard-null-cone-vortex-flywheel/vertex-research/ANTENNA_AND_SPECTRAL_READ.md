## 2. Reconcile the antenna geometry with the certified refinement [T]

A physical Sierpiński gasket retains three children out of four at each subdivision: 3ⁿ surviving triangles, edge fraction 2⁻ⁿ, retained area (3/4)ⁿ. GFD's complete Triforce ledger instead **keeps the central child**: 4ⁿ addresses. They are distinct constructions. A single face has 16 children at complete medial depth two; four faces give 64 paired addresses. The first address chooses the face; it does not halve a physical edge. Counting the face label plus two subdivisions as three address digits is consistent with Proof 23. It is not three literal physical subdivisions of each face (which would produce 4×64=256 addresses). On Proof 23's specified lattice complete depth three is not admitted. A selected time face uses exactly the 16-address slice, not all 64 as independent physical vertex modes.

With three choices designated non-gate and one gate at each of three abstract digits, the combinatorial gate-depth counts are (27,27,9,1). If the first digit designates a chosen time face, the label convention determines which count is meant. The signed pointing trace after n medial subdivisions is (3−1)ⁿ=2ⁿ. At depth two each face has 10 positive and 6 negative pointing addresses, and 1+tr(I+sQ)=17+4s for the per-face Q. These are signed traces, not a spectral dispersion law, bandwidth, or gravitation normalization. The old antenna document's 9-edge octahedron is not the regular octahedron's edge count: it has 6 vertices, 12 edges and 8 faces. A nine-record internal read may be studied separately, but the historical 6+3 wiring claim cannot be assumed as geometry. Likewise 64 paired addresses do not imply 64 physical photons, and the fine structure constant formula is a candidate, not a derived coupling. The prior nuclear synthesis uses the different law x(1−μ)²=μ; do not silently substitute it for the galactic μ=x/(1+x).

## 3. Matched spectral read [new exact capacity construction]

Let h=H₄/2 be the normalized Hadamard matrix, with one common and three contrast coordinates. A depth-two face has address space C⁴⊗C⁴. The map h⊗h is unitary; it encodes sixteen independent complex values without loss. Its grade dimensions are 1 (common-common), 3+3 (one contrast index) and 9 (two contrasts), i.e. 1+6+9=16. This also matches the chamber coordinate count 12 lateral tiles plus 4 central faces, by identifying X_ij with the off-diagonal entries and y_i with the diagonal. This is not a new 16-mode oscillator. It is a change of basis of the existing records. With a fixed time-face label, the symmetry action permutes both child indices; under C₃ the invariant dimension is (16+1+1)/3=6, with five each of the two nontrivial characters. Under S₃ the multiplicities are 5 trivial, 1 sign, 5 standard doublets. Thus a rotation-invariant scalar read would discard most of the available information.

The earlier [three-subread closure](../THREE_SUBREAD_CLOSURE.md) gives an exact reversible whole-cell tree transform 64=1+3+12+48, with 21 internal four-way readers and a coherent phase reference. The [native Walsh read](../NATIVE_WALSH_READ.md) shows why a time-history decoder need not implement an instantaneous Walsh gate. The present face read is compatible as a two-digit slice; it is not identical to that whole-cell temporal read. Three independent four-complex-channel **linear** reads have combined rank at most 12; they cannot reconstruct an arbitrary 16-complex-channel amplitude without a fourth channel block, more resolved histories, or state constraints. Four rounds of four can in principle give 16, but physical access must be derived, not inferred by counting. This is separate from three conceptual subreads, each of which can contain many records.

## 4. Resonance versus propagation [conditional model]

Self-similarity can produce matched modes on both sides, without defining a physical spatial path. If mode n has frequencies ω_A,n and ω_B,n and a common vertex coupling g_n, the two-mode Hermitian matrix [[ω_A,n,g_n],[g_n*,ω_B,n]] gives, from initial occupation entirely in A,

$$N_{B,n}(t)=\frac{|g_n|^2}{|g_n|^2+(\Delta_n/2)^2}\sin^2\left(t\sqrt{|g_n|^2+(\Delta_n/2)^2}\right),\qquad\Delta_n=\omega_{B,n}-\omega_{A,n}.$$

Subtract the common frequency and square the resulting traceless matrix to obtain the formula. It shows why spectral matching matters: at zero detuning complete exchange is possible, while a large mismatch suppresses maximum transfer. This is exact for a finite two-mode **interaction** model, not evidence of nonspatial matter transport. A linear interaction term is not necessarily a physical spatial bond; it can be the linear response of a nonspatial vertex. GFD's restriction is on the microscopic interaction architecture, not on the existence of any linear effective description.

If the wave equation and boundary conditions scale with lengths, halving all lengths doubles frequencies. A finite fractal with electromagnetic loading does not necessarily have exact dyadic eigenfrequencies. Bandwidth depends on material, geometry and radiation impedance, not just retained area. Geometry/phase matching alone cannot determine g_n or physical transfer of matter. The nonlinear vertex must provide the kernel; spectral resonance is a means of selecting which available records interact.
