## 1. One interaction, not an extra oscillator

Let χ=A† C B be the weighted overlap of the existing chamber records; equivalently χ=(WA)† K_O(WB). With real λ,κ and phase θ, take H_O=λ|χ|²+κ Re(e^{iθ}χ²), the earlier opening note's proposal generalized to the full chamber read. The time convention is i dot A=∂H/∂Ā, A=x+iy; therefore dot x=H_y/2, dot y=−H_x/2. In this convention (not the opening script's doubled real-coordinate rate),

$$\dot N_A=2\kappa\operatorname{Im}(e^{i\theta}\chi^2),\qquad\dot N_B=-\dot N_A$$

for the vertex term alone. A common phase remains a symmetry; independent phase symmetry is broken by κ unless κ=0. The gradient uses (λχ̄+κe^{iθ}χ)CB for A, and its conjugate multiplying CA for B (C Hermitian here). The λ term depends on overlap **magnitude**, not the global relative phase: it couples orientations/internal relative phases but does not by itself select a global chamber phase. For κ≠0 a relative phase can yield transfer or zero instantaneous flux. Phase locking at one time is not necessarily an equilibrium: detuning makes the phase evolve. A stationary locked trajectory needs the complete equations. The inherited scalar O coupling, when active, also contributes to each chamber occupation; include its occupation in the full conserved norm.

The pair law has a serious limitation: B=0 implies χ=0 and ∂H_O/∂B̄=0. With a separate autonomous internal H_B and no other source, exactly empty B is an invariant boundary. It cannot initiate transfer into an empty TetB from A. The same issue occurs for A=0. This does not rule out all nonlinear vertex laws: for example N Reχ is common-phase invariant and quartic, and ∂/∂B̄ at B=0 is proportional to N C A. The distinction follows from the symmetry/order of the monomial, not a guessed drain switch. A term χ² could correspond to pair exchange in a quantized model, but classical amplitudes alone do not establish particle transfer or a pair interpretation.

## 2. A constructive compatibility result [exact, not physical selection]

Let ψ=(A,B,O) be the original 33-coordinate state, H_loc the Stage VII Hamiltonian after removing all 48 direct cross-chamber hoppings (the eight inherited scalar-centre links are to the existing vertex scalar record), and E_cross=2 Reχ. Define a quartic interaction with no added state variables,

$$\mathcal H(\psi)=\psi^\dagger H_{loc}\psi + \frac{N}{N_*}E_{cross},\quad N=\|\psi\|^2.$$

N_* is the normalization of the initial sector, not a new measured physical scale. Global phase invariance conserves N. The Hamiltonian derivative is H_loc ψ+(N/N_*)H_cross ψ+(E_cross/N_*)ψ, where H_cross is the matrix with C in its off-diagonal blocks. On N=N_* the first two terms give exactly Stage VII; the last is a real scalar times ψ. Multiplication by a time-dependent common phase removes it. Explicitly if ψ_lin(t)=exp(−itH_VII)ψ(0), then ψ(t)=exp[−i∫₀ᵗ E_cross(ψ_lin(s))/N_* ds] ψ_lin(t). All phase-neutral observables, including ring currents and the effective cross-channel decomposition, are identical on this norm shell. For general N the cross strength scales as N/N_*. This constructs an exact compatibility witness on disjoint labelled records, including arbitrary states, not only the collective triplet. It has a nonlinear functional globally but on a fixed norm is projectively the old linear model: it is not evidence of genuine nonlinear relative dynamics there. It deliberately imports C. Infinitely many other functions F(N) with F(N_*)=1 have the same projective dynamics, so the old model cannot determine the nonlinear law uniquely.

This shows that the geometry and existing dynamics can be embedded in a single quartic vertex interaction without a physical cross-chamber spatial path. It does **not** derive the 48 numbers from the tetrad, explain why nature picks C, or establish that occupation means matter, gravitation or causal transmission. The original proofs need no mathematical change.

## 3. A genuinely nonlinear candidate and its limits [numerical]

Use H_O=κ Reχ², θ=0, λ=0, with the original Stage VII C, H_loc and normalized row-closed inversion-even initial condition z=(1,i,0)/√2, (u,v,w)=(1/3,1,0), ρ=√3/8. Exact transpose expectation on the row-closed pattern is (8/9)||q||²; since ||q||²=4 and d=−1/2, initial χ=−1/12. Choose κ=1/χ_initial=−12 solely to match the original initial derivative (not derived or calibrated physics). In the invariant balanced sector A=B, χ is real, and the effective coefficient multiplying C becomes κχ(t). The three-amplitude reduction therefore becomes i dot x=(M_A+κχ(x)M_C)x. This is genuinely nonlinear relative evolution, even at fixed norm. Its core is not a separately driven oscillator. All eight rings can feed central-face occupation through the vertex read. No scalar O amplitude appears in this sector; its darkness does not imply the interaction itself is absent.

The verifier integrates this invariant reduction and independently integrates the full 33-coordinate system. For dimensionless t=0..20 with 401 samples, the reduced candidate's core is occupied at all 400 sampled positive times, with maximum occupation approximately 0.007827 (Stage VII about 0.101100 in this sample). The minimum absolute individual ring current across the sampled trajectory is about 0.056998 (Stage VII about 0.039931). The cross read's norm is scaled by |κχ|; no physical core-bond flow is asserted. The balanced branch has N_A=N_B throughout, so it has **zero net chamber transfer even while the central vortex read is nonzero**. This is a valuable counterexample to equating primary circulation with drainage. The nonzero-time result is sampled, not an analytic all-time theorem. Compare the plot and CSV.

The unrestricted nonlinear full-space integration conserves norm and energy at the stated tolerance and agrees with the invariant solution initially, but accumulates transverse deviations: max chamber-parity discrepancy ≈0.564 and final vector difference ≈1.546. Roundoff/truncation perturbations can excite other sectors; this is evidence of sensitivity, not a rigorous instability exponent. The exact symmetry-invariant subspace remains a theorem, but full-space nonlinear stability is **not** certified. Do not promote this candidate to a sustained physical vortex theorem. The norm-lift witness has no such relative nonlinear dynamics and matches the original full-state integration projectively.

## 4. A drain and a gravitational response are different conditions

No net transfer does not imply B empty. It also does not imply no static TetB gravitational susceptibility. The [net-field proof](../../../proofs/net-field-rule/) applies under its drainless ownership premise; it is not a microscopic rule setting N_B=0. With an open drain a field can transfer a conserved quantity to TetB via the vertex, but in a finite closed system the time-integrated net transfer equals the bounded change in occupation (plus any vertex storage). It cannot sustain a strictly positive long-time mean net flux indefinitely. A maintained drain needs an accounted source, sink or larger return network. The Field Origin can mediate resonance and transfer in one law, yet irreversible settlement and the physical definition of drain still need an action and boundary conditions.

Even if μ=x/(x+1) is used as the external response relation for draining fields, s=2μ−1 is a continuous state share, not the orientation eigenvalue of an individual tetrad. At finite x the expression 1−μ is nonzero. A theorem that a drainless configuration yields zero local corrective response must derive a response/read operator and its vanishing on the drainless branch, not insert μ or identify zero occupation flux with zero gravitational correction. The earlier [read–settle–register bridge](../../read-settle-register-bridge/READ_SETTLE_REGISTER_BRIDGE.md) gives a conditional time-leg torsion response, while [where the roots live](../../bimond-gfd/14_where_the_roots_live.md) separates axial circulation from the force channel. The new vertex should eventually supply the coupling between these reads. A global time parameter used in the finite Hamiltonian is also not yet a derivation of the two time faces' temporal synchronization or relativistic causality.
