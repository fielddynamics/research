## The three degree-2 cyclotomic polynomials

$$\Phi_3(K) \;=\; K^2 + K + 1$$
$$\Phi_4(K) \;=\; K^2 + 1$$
$$\Phi_6(K) \;=\; K^2 - K + 1$$

These are the three cyclotomic polynomials of degree exactly 2 over $\mathbb Q$. They are the minimal polynomials of the primitive 3rd, 4th, and 6th roots of unity respectively.

**Identity (immediate):** $\Phi_3(K) - \Phi_6(K) = 2K$, and $\Phi_3(K) + \Phi_6(K) = 2(K^2 + 1) = 2\Phi_4(K)$.

$$\boxed{\;\Phi_6(4) \;=\; 13,\qquad \Phi_4(4) \;=\; 17,\qquad \Phi_3(4) \;=\; 21\;}$$

These three integers are the framework's primary algebraic primitives. Every load-bearing quantity in the framework is built from $\{13, 17, 21\}$.

## Why degree 2

Only the cyclotomic polynomials $\Phi_3, \Phi_4, \Phi_6$ have degree exactly 2 over $\mathbb Q$. The next-lowest degrees are 1 ($\Phi_1 = K - 1$, $\Phi_2 = K + 1$) and 4 ($\Phi_5, \Phi_8, \Phi_{10}, \Phi_{12}$).

The degree-2 condition selects polynomials whose evaluation at integer $K$ gives quadratic integer expressions $K^2 + bK + c$ with $b \in \{-1, 0, 1\}$ and $c = 1$. These three polynomials are the only minimal polynomials of primitive roots of unity matching this form.

The framework's tetrad bilinearity ($g_{\mu\nu} = \eta_{ab} e^a_\mu e^b_\nu$ is bilinear in $e$) selects degree-2 algebraic content from $\mathbb R^K$. The three cyclotomic polynomials of degree 2 are the natural Galois-theoretic image of this bilinearity at the algebraic level.

## The trace formula

The three integers $\{13, 17, 21\}$ arise structurally as traces of the graded identity operator on the closure-read representation:

$$\operatorname{Tr}(\mathbb I_s) \;=\; 1 + sK + K^2$$

for $s \in \{+1, 0, -1\}$ — see `01.3_graded_identity_operator.md`.

At $K = 4$:

$$\operatorname{Tr}(\mathbb I_{+1}) \;=\; 21 \;=\; \Phi_3(4)$$
$$\operatorname{Tr}(\mathbb I_{0}) \;=\; 17 \;=\; \Phi_4(4)$$
$$\operatorname{Tr}(\mathbb I_{-1}) \;=\; 13 \;=\; \Phi_6(4)$$

**This is the connection between cyclotomic polynomials and parity-graded characters.** The trace of $\mathbb I_s$ at $s = +1, 0, -1$ produces exactly the three degree-2 cyclotomic polynomial values.
