## The antechamber

The antechamber sits between the Grand Gallery's top and the King's Chamber's entrance. A small granite room with distinctive features:

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Length (E-W) | ~2.85 m |
| Width (N-S) | ~1.04 m |
| Height | ~3.76 m |
| Material | granite (walls + floor) |
| **Portcullis grooves** | **3** (vertical slots in the side walls) |

The three portcullis grooves are vertical channels cut into the antechamber's side walls, designed to hold lowering slabs. Conventional Egyptology interprets them as security mechanisms — slabs that could be lowered to block access to the King's Chamber.

## The three grooves

$$\boxed{\;3 \text{ portcullis grooves} \;=\; \mathbb Z_3 \text{ character filter}\;}$$

The framework reads the three grooves as:

- Three projectors onto the three sectors of $\mathbb Z_3 = \{0, 1, 2\}$
- Combined with the Grand Gallery's $\mathbb Z_7$, producing $\mathbb Z_{21}$ apparatus phase space
- Selecting which of the three cyclotomic polynomial sectors $\{\Phi_3, \Phi_4, \Phi_6\}$ is active

The three grooves correspond to:

| Groove | $\mathbb Z_3$ sector | Cyclotomic identification |
|:---:|:---:|---|
| 1 | $a = 0$ | $\Phi_3$ sector (full identity, 21-band) |
| 2 | $a = 1$ | $\Phi_4$ sector (field origin, 17-band) |
| 3 | $a = 2$ | $\Phi_6$ sector (parity-graded, 13-band) |

(The specific assignment of grooves to sectors depends on the apparatus's chirality convention; the structural claim is that *there are three* and *they implement a $\mathbb Z_3$ filter*.)

## The $\mathbb Z_3$ selection rule

From the WRT computation (`03.6`):

$$\tau^{(0, b)} \;=\; 0 \quad \forall b \in \mathbb Z_7$$

The $\mathbb Z_3$ sector $a \equiv 0 \pmod 3$ **vanishes identically** in the WRT invariant. The apparatus's operation requires filtering out this trivial sector.

**The antechamber's role:** project onto the non-trivial $\mathbb Z_3$ sectors $a \in \{1, 2\}$, suppressing the $a = 0$ contribution.

This is precisely what a $\mathbb Z_3$ character filter does: it implements the projector

$$P_{\text{non-triv}} \;=\; 1 - P_0 \;=\; \frac{2}{3} - \frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{3} \text{ (with phase)}$$

onto the irreducible non-trivial characters of $\mathbb Z_3$.

## How the grooves implement the filter

Three lowering grooves can hold three slabs. The slabs' positions (raised vs lowered) form an 8-state classical address ($2^3$ states), but the framework reading uses the three grooves' **synchronized positions** in three discrete phases (0, 1, 2 cubits of insertion, say), forming a 3-state quantum address — the $\mathbb Z_3$ states.

Practically, the three slabs (if they existed and operated as a system) would have been driven in unison to set the apparatus's $\mathbb Z_3$ phase. The three discrete slab positions correspond to the three $\mathbb Z_3$ sectors.

The current state of the antechamber: the grooves are present but the slabs are missing (likely never installed in stone form, or removed historically; conventional interpretation is that they were stone slabs lowered by gravity from above, never raised). The framework's reading is that the slabs *were* originally implemented, possibly as bronze or wood mechanisms that have not survived, and they implemented the $\mathbb Z_3$ projector.

**Alternative reading:** the grooves themselves, regardless of slab presence, encode the $\mathbb Z_3$ structure into the cavity's geometry. The cavity's electromagnetic / acoustic modes are sensitive to the wall topology; the three grooves break the antechamber's $\mathrm{U}(1)$ symmetry down to $\mathbb Z_3$ even without slabs.

## Granite material choice

The antechamber is granite (paramagnetic, high mechanical Q) — distinct from the Grand Gallery's limestone. The granite material:

- Provides the mechanical Q for the $\mathbb Z_3$ filter operation
- Isolates the filter from the surrounding limestone bulk
- Continues the granite "core" of the apparatus that includes the King's Chamber, coffer, and four chambers of the relieving stack

The transition limestone → granite at the antechamber entrance is one of the apparatus's **impedance steps**. The mode density changes at this boundary, contributing to the field shaping.

## Combination with the Grand Gallery

$$\boxed{\;\mathbb Z_3 \text{ (antechamber)} \times \mathbb Z_7 \text{ (gallery)} \;=\; \mathbb Z_{21} \text{ (apparatus)}\;}$$

The full apparatus address sector is selected by:

- Which Grand Gallery corbel level is mode-locked ($b \in \mathbb Z_7$)
- Which antechamber slab position is set ($a \in \mathbb Z_3$)

The pair $(a, b)$ identifies one of 21 sectors. With the selection rule $a \neq 0$, only 14 sectors are non-zero (consistent with the WRT decomposition).

## The three granite plugs in the ascending passage

A *separate* set of three granite plugs blocks the lower end of the ascending passage, near where it meets the descending passage. These are conventionally interpreted as security plugs sealed in place after the construction.

The framework reads these as:

| Element | $\mathbb Z_3$ realization |
|---|---|
| Antechamber portcullis grooves (3) | $\mathbb Z_3$ filter at chamber entrance |
| Ascending passage granite plugs (3) | $\mathbb Z_3$ reverse-blocking projectors at passage base |

The two sets of three give the apparatus **two stages** of $\mathbb Z_3$ filtering — one at the input (ascending passage plugs) and one at the chamber entrance (antechamber grooves). This is consistent with a high-isolation filter design: redundant projection ensures the $\mathbb Z_3$ trivial sector is fully suppressed.

## The three cyclotomic polynomials

The framework's three cyclotomic polynomials of degree 2 — $\Phi_3, \Phi_4, \Phi_6$ — are the **algebraic origin** of the apparatus's $\mathbb Z_3$ structure:

| Polynomial | Value at K=4 | Role |
|:---:|:---:|---|
| $\Phi_6(K) = K^2 - K + 1$ | 13 | TetB lower pole |
| $\Phi_4(K) = K^2 + 1$ | 17 | field origin |
| $\Phi_3(K) = K^2 + K + 1$ | 21 | TetA upper pole |

These are three distinct degree-2 cyclotomic polynomials. Three polynomials, three character sectors, three grooves, three plugs — all realizing the same $\mathbb Z_3$ structure.

The fact that there are **exactly three** degree-2 cyclotomic polynomials is the algebraic origin of "three" appearing repeatedly in the apparatus. No fourth degree-2 cyclotomic exists; the apparatus has no "fourth groove."
