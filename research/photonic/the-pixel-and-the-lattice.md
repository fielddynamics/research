## Start with Water

Before we talk about light or gravity or the structure of reality, think about water.

A single water molecule is a complete object. It has a shape — two hydrogen atoms bonded to one oxygen at 104.5 degrees. It has internal structure. It has properties. It exists on its own.

But a single water molecule is not water.

Water is what happens when many molecules organize. They form hydrogen bonds. They arrange into a lattice. In ice, that lattice is hexagonal — six molecules forming a ring, tiling across a plane, stacking into crystals. The hexagonal pattern is not imposed from outside. It emerges from the bond angle of the individual molecule. The shape of the part determines the pattern of the whole.

A water vortex — a whirlpool — is a coherent structure within that lattice. It is not a separate thing added to the water. It is water, organized into a rotating pattern. The individual molecule does not travel around the drain. The pattern does. The vortex has a shape, a velocity profile, a throat where the flow narrows. It is made entirely of water molecules, but it has properties that no single molecule possesses.

Now replace "water molecule" with "photon." Replace "water" with "spacetime." Replace "vortex" with "gravity field."

The analogy is not a metaphor. It is the physics.
