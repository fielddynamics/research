## Vortex filament nonlinearity

- H. Hasimoto, "A soliton on a vortex filament," *J. Fluid Mech.* 51, 477–485, 1972. Local induction approximation ↔ cubic nonlinear Schrödinger equation.
- Hopfinger and Browand (1982) observed Hasimoto-type solitary waves on vortex filaments in a rotating container experiment, as cited in R. A. Van Gorder, arXiv:1402.7023.
