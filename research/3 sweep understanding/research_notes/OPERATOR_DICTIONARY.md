# Operator dictionary and finite proofs

This is the mathematical companion to the [main README](../README.md). We use different symbols for different operators rather than overloading “sweep.” All claims are conditional on the specified representations.

## A. Inherited algebra and audit

The elementary state group is (Z/2)^3, with characters χ_b(a)=(-1)^popcount(a&b). Character orthogonality yields H_n H_n^T=nI. The Clifford sign cocycle twists the group algebra into a specified Clifford product; ordinary XOR composition and signed Clifford multiplication are not identical. A Boolean operator represented by Walsh coefficients can be applied through the inverse read, with thresholding only if a Boolean output is intended. Sequential compositions of thresholded layers are not automatically just pointwise multiplication of their coefficients. An order-three integer module has an Eisenstein action on the subspace annihilated by Ω²+Ω+I. The fixed part and the cyclic part depend on the actual Ω.

The legacy pipeline uses a 2+6 axial/hexagonal geometric split. A literal cyclic permutation of the three vector axes and dual bivector axes also fixes their two sums, in addition to scalar and pseudoscalar. It has a 4-dimensional fixed part on Cl(3,0), not two. Thus the older 2+6 claim needs a specified different order-three action; the geometry of six projected hexagon vertices does not resolve this ambiguity. The legacy files are retained verbatim for provenance, not silently corrected. Similarly, s²<4 is equivalent to the quadratic having nonreal roots, not to irreducibility over Q in general: K²+3K+1 is irreducible with real roots. The three degree-two cyclotomic polynomials remain exactly Φ₃, Φ₄, Φ₆. A finite order-three symmetry does not itself terminate arbitrary dynamical recursion. A projective plane point count requires K² to be a prime power; for K=4, it is 16. The equality Φ₃(−K)=Φ₃(K−1)=Φ₆(K) follows by expansion; broader uniqueness claims need their own proof. The connection split 4·10+4·6=64 is exact combinatorics, not by itself an identification with a specific affine connection action.

## B. Tree/FWHT theorem

Let X be a real or complex 4×4×4 tensor, h=H4/2. Apply h along c for every a,b; next along b with c=0; last along a with b=c=0. Call the product B. Each operation is orthogonal (unitary for complex data) and acts on disjoint four-entry blocks within a stage. The coefficient with a=b=c=0 is Σ X/8, by three successive factors 1/2. At fine stage retain 16×3 differences; at intermediate stage 4×3; at coarse stage 3. Every output is retained. Hence B is invertible and preserves norm. Define D to act as h⊗h on (a,b) with c≠0 and h on a where c=0,b≠0. In each such stratum, D supplies exactly the transforms omitted by B; operations on different digits commute before restriction. Thus DB=h⊗h⊗h. The code tests the equality on arbitrary inputs and compares against six binary butterfly stages.

## C. Prefix flag and signed character

On the four-symbol alphabet cycle 1→2→3→1 and fix 0. Apply it simultaneously to d digits. Burnside gives (4^d+2)/3 orbits. The prefix-constant projections commute with the cyclic average. The nested invariant prefix ranks are 1,2,6,22; their orthogonal differences have ranks 1,4,16. Let G_d be these differences. Then A_s=G1+sG2+G3 has trace 17+4s. On the 21-dimensional contrast space A_+ is I, A_- is an involution with 17 positive and 4 negative eigenvalues, and A_0=(A_++A_-)/2. Their ranks are 21,21,17 respectively (ordered +,−,0), not 21,13,17. The 17/13 ratio is a ratio of traces. The choice of the middle grade and coupling to physical chamber orientation remain premises. The 273 product identity follows (K²+1)²−K²=K⁴+K²+1; the lower-triangular Sierpiński blocks have 1+9+81=91 nonzero entries in the specified construction.

## D. XOR pair invariance, seed and daughter masks

R(a,b)=(b,a⊕b) fixes only (0,0) and cycles every other pair. On 64 addresses the invariant subspace H has 22 dimensions. Exchange J(a,b)=(b,a) has even/odd dimensions 15/7 on H: the zero and seven dependent triples are fixed, while the independent triples are paired. The symplectic Walsh read W uses exponent a·d+b·c for pair (c,d), normalized by 8; W²=I and W commutes with R and J because they preserve the alternating form. The ordinary tensor FWHT uses a·c+b·d and is related by a coordinate exchange, not identical without that convention.

The common seed f=1[a=0]+1[b=0]+1[a=b] has squared norm 30. Each support is a self-annihilating 3-space, so W fixes its indicator, and R,J permute the supports. On the invariant space, dependent records consist of the zero orbit plus seven nonzero rank-one orbits. An independent pair spans one Fano plane; each of seven planes contains six ordered generating pairs, split into two directed R orbits. For nonzero u, Q_u includes all eight dependent-orbit records and both directed orbits of the three Fano planes through u: rank 14. The common line f is included, so Q_u−Fseed is an orthogonal rank-13 projector. For two axes there is a unique Fano plane containing both, so the common raw rank is 10 and the reduced rank 9. If u,v,w form a binary line, the same plane occurs in all pair intersections and the triple intersection; inclusion-exclusion gives 3·13−3·9+9=21. Otherwise the triple intersection has reduced rank 7 and the union is 19. The Heaven normal h=111 has perpendicular nonzero vectors 3,5,6, a binary line. This proves the cumulative ladder in any ordering. The actual sets are in `outputs/mask_membership.csv`.

The uniform mean vector in normalized orbit coordinates is sqrt(w)/8 with w=(1,3,...,3). It is not f/√30. The seed removal is appropriate for these masks; weighted mean removal is appropriate for the independently known mean innovation problem. No physical equivalence is assumed. Removing the zero address alone is not Walsh invariant.

## E. Continuous orientation and reversible reset

For Q=Q_7, S=Q+(I−Q)J on H. This is an orthogonal involution. Let C=SWSW, M=(2I−C−C²)/3, A=(C−C²)/√3. In this specified model C³=I and M has rank four, verified by the new tests. Because C^T=C², M²=M, A^T=−A, A²=−M, AM=MA=A. Therefore U(θ)=I+(cosθ−1)M+sinθ A is orthogonal and fixes the common seed. Its contrast trace is 17+4cosθ. The endpoint values 13 and 21 and midpoint 17 do not mean S=U(π); the test disproves equality. Native C3 phase samples have traces 21,15,15. A controller can reset a coordinate convention at zero cost in a passive model; a physical active reset must account for energy H and satisfy J†HJ=H for state-independent energy preservation. No autonomous clock is derived by writing U(θ).

For each orbit triple, transform (x1,x2,x3) to ((x1+x2+x3)/√3,(2x1−x2−x3)/√6,(x2−x3)/√2). The three rows are orthonormal, so the inverse is the transpose. The 21 triples and one fixed point produce 22 means and 42 contrasts. Interpret phase only in a canonical cyclic order or with the arrival permutation retained. For arbitrary evolving records, use a common-time reference before combining them. A reset may reuse scratch space after output or preserve state in-place; erasing arbitrary data cannot be reversible.

## F. Certified BBP address walk and Walsh streaming

The known BBP series is π=Σ_{k≥0}16^(−k)[4/(8k+1)−2/(8k+4)−1/(8k+5)−1/(8k+6)]. The bracket equals the integral over y∈[0,1] of y^(8k)(4−2y³−y⁴−y⁵), nonnegative and at most 4/(8k+1). Thus the tail after N terms is bounded by 64/[15(8N+1)16^N]. The verifier uses N=270 and exact rational arithmetic; both bounds floor to the same integer after scaling by 8^332. This certifies the octal prefix used, rather than assuming the last digit correct.

Start at v=3, update u=(v+d) mod 8, record (v,u). The cyclic translation has order eight, unlike XOR shifts; the Walsh transform of the cyclic increment has off-diagonal Frobenius norm √5. On an arbitrary fixed table X, add X_ab h_a h_b^T once per first visit, where h_a is normalized H8 column a. Linearity gives H8 X H8 when all 64 pairs are present. Repeated visits add nothing in this estimator. An alternative weighted visit average divides by per-address counts, not the total alone. A full table is covered at step 332. If the state changes across steps, mixed-time samples generally do not give one instantaneous transform.

The 333-digit count includes the leading digit; there are 332 transitions, not 333. The actual bank completions and mismatch with daughter sets are tested. A discounted base-8 ledger L_ab=Σ_{n:pair=(a,b)}8^(-n) preserves order, unlike raw counts. With decoder D_ab=(b−a) mod 8, π_N=3+Σ D_ab L_ab. The repository establishes a 16-nonzero-entry spectral decoder H8 D H8 /64 with unnormalized H8. It is a property of the chosen decoder, not a derivation of physical tetrad coordinates.

## G. Weighted innovations and determinant

Let orbit-constant values b have weighted inner product diag(w) with w=(1,3,...,3). For unread set U, R_U=diag(1_U)−1_U(w 1_U)^T/Σ_Uw. This projects onto supported vectors with zero weighted sum, and R_empty=0. With known mean, L_U=I−P_mean−R_U resolves independent contrasts. On reading i, the innovation D_i=R_U−R_(U\{i}) is an orthogonal projector by nesting. If |U|>1 its rank is one; if |U|=1 it is zero (the last value is already inferred). Different increments are orthogonal; their sum is I−P_mean. The actual walk attains rank 21 at 104. The code implements this in normalized coordinates and verifies all 210 pairwise innovation orthogonalities.

For scalar t, T_i=I+(t−1)D_i. Orthogonal increments imply product T_i=I+(t−1)L. The nontrivial singular values are |t| with multiplicity rank L; hence det(T†T)=|t|^(2 rank L). This derivation establishes a determinant law under a particular elementary attenuation choice; it does not establish that physical work is this determinant. Ordinary contrast energy transmission for one pass is |t|². The common mode is untouched. A lossless dilation with complementary reflected modes is possible for |t|≤1, but does not derive t from the native field.

## H. Separate physical and geometric symmetry

A spatial 120° cube rotation has two fixed corners; on ordered corner pairs it therefore has (64+2·4)/3=24 invariant dimensions. The internal XOR/Pauli action has 22. No equivariant relabeling can identify them. The recursive Triforce tree has one fixed leaf under the prescribed simultaneous rotation, also giving 22; equality of dimensions is not a physical isomorphism. A 64-coordinate Pauli operator expansion of an 8-component coherent core is a different ledger from a real address FWHT, even though both admit four-way transforms. Three-qubit Pauli rotation U3 cycles X,Z,Y by conjugation. It maps XXX to ZZZ, while the native free core compression commutes with inversion XXX. Thus native free propagation cannot equal this cycle for arbitrary states. A controlled pulse can realize it, but the required controls are not shown to emerge autonomously from the vortices.
