## 2. The unique minimum-trace positive reciprocal completion

Assume a *static* reciprocal quadratic action with fixed real symmetric C and equal chamber self-block D. Positivity requires H=[[D,C],[C,D]]>=0, equivalently D+C>=0 and D-C>=0 by orthogonal even/odd transformation. In the eigenbasis of C, D_ii>=|lambda_i|, hence tr D>=tr |C|. D=|C| attains the bound. For any minimizer, each D_ii=|lambda_i|. For each i one of D+C or D-C has zero i-th diagonal, and a positive semidefinite matrix with zero diagonal has its entire corresponding row zero. Thus D_ij=0 for all i!=j in that basis. The minimizer is unique, D=|C|=(C^2)^(1/2). For invertible C the full block is semidefinite with rank 16, not a strictly stable positive stiffness. A strict variant with off-diagonal eta C, |eta|<1, and the same D is positive definite but does not minimize trace for fixed eta C. This is a new conditional minimum-trace principle; neither the native Hamiltonian nor physical time-face action derives it.
