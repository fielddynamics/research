## The shell Hamiltonians

For each frame level $K$, define the shell Hamiltonian on the K-level Hilbert space:

$$\boxed{\;H_K \;=\; \Phi_6(K)\,\Pi_{K,-} \;+\; \Phi_4(K)\,\Pi_{K,0} \;+\; \Phi_3(K)\,\Pi_{K,+}\;}$$

where $\Pi_{K,-}, \Pi_{K,0}, \Pi_{K,+}$ are projectors onto the three eigenspaces of the K-level shell, with eigenvalues given by the three cyclotomic polynomials at $K$.

The three eigenstates are labeled by the signature $s \in \{-, 0, +\}$ corresponding to the parity-graded read at signatures $\{-1, 0, +1\}$.

## Explicit forms

**At K=4:**

$$H_4 \;=\; 13\,\Pi_{4,-} \;+\; 17\,\Pi_{4,0} \;+\; 21\,\Pi_{4,+}$$

Eigenvalues: $\{13, 17, 21\}$ corresponding to {TetB lower pole, field origin, TetA upper pole}.

**At K=5:**

$$H_5 \;=\; 21\,\Pi_{5,-} \;+\; 26\,\Pi_{5,0} \;+\; 31\,\Pi_{5,+}$$

Eigenvalues: $\{21, 26, 31\}$ corresponding to {K=5 lower pole, K=5 mid-pole, K=5 upper pole}.

**Observation:** $H_4$'s top eigenvalue (21) equals $H_5$'s bottom eigenvalue (21). This is the algebraic shift identity (`01.4`) realized at the operator level.

## The interlevel hinge operator

Define:

$$\boxed{\;J_4 \;=\; |5,-\rangle\langle 4,+|\;}$$

This is the rank-1 operator that takes the K=4 upper pole eigenstate to the K=5 lower pole eigenstate.

## The intertwiner equation

**Theorem:** $J_4$ intertwines $H_4$ and $H_5$ with eigenvalue 21:

$$\boxed{\;H_5 J_4 \;=\; J_4 H_4 \;=\; 21\, J_4\;}$$

**Proof.**

LHS: $H_5 J_4 = H_5 |5,-\rangle\langle 4,+| = \Phi_6(5)\,|5,-\rangle\langle 4,+| = 21\,J_4$.

RHS: $J_4 H_4 = |5,-\rangle\langle 4,+|\,H_4 = |5,-\rangle\,\Phi_3(4)\,\langle 4,+| = 21\,J_4$. $\square$

The eigenvalue 21 on both sides is the same integer $\Phi_3(4) = \Phi_6(5)$. **The hinge intertwines because the bands are equal.**

## Geometric content

The intertwiner equation says:

$$\boxed{\;J_4\text{ transfers the K=4 upper pole into the K=5 lower pole with no band mismatch.}\;}$$

This is the operator-level realization of the cyclotomic shift identity $\Phi_6(K+1) = \Phi_3(K)$. The shift identity gives the algebraic match; the intertwiner gives the operator realization.

## The Hermitian conjugate hinge

The reverse hinge:

$$J_4^\dagger \;=\; |4,+\rangle\langle 5,-|$$

satisfies (by the same calculation):

$$H_4 J_4^\dagger \;=\; J_4^\dagger H_5 \;=\; 21\, J_4^\dagger$$

The pair $(J_4, J_4^\dagger)$ implements the portal cycle bidirectionally.

## The portal state cycle

The complete portal operation cycles:

$$\boxed{\;|x, 4, +\rangle \;\xrightarrow{J_4(x)}\; |x, 5, -\rangle \;\xrightarrow{\mathcal M(U,V)}\; |y, 5, -\rangle \;\xrightarrow{J_4^\dagger(y)}\; |y, 4, +\rangle\;}$$

Step 1: local hinge fires at $x$, lifting K=4 upper pole into K=5 lower pole.

Step 2: mirror selector $\mathcal M(U,V)$ projects the K=5 state at $x$ onto the matching K=5 state at $y$.

Step 3: reverse hinge fires at $y$, lowering K=5 lower pole back to K=4 upper pole.

**Net effect:** state transfer from $x$ to $y$ through the 21-band coupling, mediated by the mirror selector.

The K=5 pentachoron has $\binom{5}{4} = 5$ sub-tetrahedral cells, one for each excluded vertex. The hinge $J_4$ therefore has a 5-fold multiplicity:

$$J_4 \;=\; \sum_{v=1}^{5} J_4^{(v)}\, \pi_v$$

where $\pi_v$ projects onto the sub-tetrahedron excluding vertex $v$, and $J_4^{(v)}$ is the hinge from the K=4 sub-tetrahedron $\{1, \dots, 5\}\setminus\{v\}$ into the K=5 shell.

This 5-fold multiplicity is the $S_5 \supset S_4$ branching of the framework's frame group. It is combinatorially forced, not metaphorical.
