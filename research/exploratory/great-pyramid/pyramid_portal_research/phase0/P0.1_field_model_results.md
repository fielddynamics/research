## Study A — the GFD closure current

### Model

The closure current of `01.7` is the Noether current of the scalar sector, $\mathbf J = \mu\nabla\Phi/4\pi G$. Inside the pyramid $g/a_0 \sim 10^{11}$, so μ = 1 to one part in 10¹¹ and $\mathbf J \propto \nabla\Phi$. The field threading the structure has three parts:

- **The terrestrial field.** $g = 9.793$ m/s², vertical.
- **Its free-air gradient.** $3.086 \times 10^{-6}$ s⁻², so $g$ falls by 4.6 × 10⁻⁵ of its value over the pyramid's height.
- **The pyramid's self-gravity.** A uniform-density square pyramid (ρ = 2300 kg/m³, Petrie's dimensions) discretised into ~2 × 10⁴ cells with softened point-mass sums. At the base centre it is 5.0 × 10⁻⁶ $g$.

The flux $\Phi_\Sigma(z)$ through each horizontal section is integrated by 20 × 20 Gauss–Legendre quadrature. The half-flux plane $\Phi_\Sigma(z) = \tfrac12\Phi_{\text{base}}$ is found by bisection.

### Uniformity margin

`01.7` requires the axial variation of the closure field to satisfy $|\varepsilon| \lesssim 1.3 \times 10^{-3}$. The modelled field varies by 4.6 × 10⁻⁵, from the free-air gradient, plus a 5.0 × 10⁻⁶ self-gravity correction. That is a margin of ~30. The balance plane is therefore geometric to within 0.02 in. **The King's Chamber floor coincides with the structure's local Field Origin to 0.82 in, with no adjustable input.**

### Pointwise μ and structural μ_B

At every point inside the pyramid the pointwise constitutive response is μ(x) ≈ 1: the terrestrial field is deep in the TetA regime. The structural closure fraction $\mu_B(z) = \Phi_\Sigma(z)/\Phi_{\text{base}}$ of `01.7` is a different quantity. It measures how the closure current threading the body divides between the share continuing along the axis and the share exchanged through the faces, and it runs from 1 at the base to 0 at the apex. Because the field is uniform to 5 × 10⁻⁵, μ_B is set by the geometry of the body alone. That is the scale-invariant statement of `01.6`: the local Field Origin of a bounded closure is a property of its form.

## Study B — the atmospheric pump

### Model

The pyramid is replaced by the cone of equal height (146.71 m) and equal base area (radius 129.96 m), standing on a grounded plane, in a uniform fair-weather field $E_0 = 130$ V/m. The axisymmetric Laplace equation $\nabla\cdot(\varepsilon\nabla V) = 0$ is solved by finite differences on a 900 m × 900 m domain, with grid spacings 4, 2 and 1 m.

| Case | Body | Use |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | dielectric, $\varepsilon_r = 8$ (dry limestone) | how the pump field threads the body |
| B2 | grounded conductor (DC steady state; a gilded or moist skin) | apex enhancement, current and power |

### B1 — the pump field does not locate the Field Origin

The flux of $D_z$ through horizontal sections is half its base value at:

| Grid | 4 m | 2 m | 1 m |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-flux half plane | 63.5 m | 61.8 m | 61.0 m |

That is about 18 m above $z^*$ (42.97 m). The pump field enters the body through its sloping faces, so its flux is not axially uniform, and its balance plane does not fall at $z^*$.

This sharpens the model rather than weakening it:

- **The GFD closure current locates the Field Origin.** The scalar Noether current threads the body uniformly and balances at the King's Chamber floor (Study A).
- **The electromagnetic pump does not.** It supplies energy but has no role in setting the location.

The two roles, location and energisation, are carried by different fields, as the framework's split between the scalar (gravitational) sector and the vector (EM) sector of the action implies.

### B2 — apex enhancement, current and power

| Quantity | 4 m grid | 2 m grid | 1 m grid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apex field enhancement (first node above the apex) | 6.2 | 9.4 | 13.4 |
| Capture area | 1.098 × 10⁵ m² | 1.099 × 10⁵ m² | 1.102 × 10⁵ m² |
| Extractable power bound, fair weather | 1.047 mW | 1.048 mW | 1.051 mW |

- **Apex enhancement.** The apex of a conducting cone is a field singularity, so the enhancement rises without limit as the grid is refined. The 1 m value, ≥ 13, is a lower bound. A gilded tip of centimetre radius raises the local field by orders of magnitude more, toward the corona threshold of air.
- **Capture area.** The earthed structure gathers the atmospheric field from **1.10 × 10⁵ m²**, 2.1 times its footprint. This figure is converged.
- **Current.** With the fair-weather air–earth current density of 2 pA/m², the collected current is **0.22 μA**.
- **Power.** If the apex is insulated so that it floats toward the ambient potential at its height ($E_0 h \approx 19$ kV), a matched load extracts at most $\tfrac14 I E_0 h \approx$ **1 mW** in fair weather.
- **Dust storms.** Desert dust storms raise the potential gradient to ~10 kV/m and more, with currents scaling roughly with the field. The bound scales as $E_0^2$, to **≈ 6 W** at 10 kV/m.

### What B2 means for the energy budget

The atmospheric pump delivers power on the scale of **milliwatts in fair weather and watts in storms**. This is the first quantitative input to the energy budget of `08.4`. Any operating mode of the structure must either work within this power, or draw on the other channels `08.4` lists: the address coupling, or the TetB sheet itself. The natural next calculation compares this figure with the energy required to drive the King's Chamber's principal acoustic modes to a given amplitude, once the acoustic model (H2, H3) supplies their Q.
