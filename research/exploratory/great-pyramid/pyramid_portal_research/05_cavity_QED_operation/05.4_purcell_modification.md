# 05.4 — Purcell Modification of Local Coupling

**Status:** Structural (Purcell factor formula applied; numerical magnitude depends on open parameter)
**Reading time:** 4 minutes

## The claim

Inside the King's Chamber, the apparatus's strong-coupling cavity **modifies the local universal-vertex coupling** by a Purcell factor. The apparatus produces a **measurable local deviation in gravitational coupling** inside the cavity, distinct from the bulk $\alpha_G$ outside:

$$\boxed{\;\frac{\alpha_G^{\text{local}}}{\alpha_G^{\text{bulk}}} \;=\; 1 \,+\, F_P\;}$$

where $F_P$ is the Purcell enhancement factor. The sign and magnitude of $F_P$ are derivable from cavity-QED standard theory, subject to one open parameter (the transition frequency $\omega_{K4 \to K5}$, file 08.1).

This is the apparatus's **measurable local-gravity prediction**: a precision gravimeter inside the chamber should read a different value of $G$ than outside.

## The Purcell factor

For an emitter in a single-mode cavity, the Purcell factor is:

$$F_P \;=\; \frac{3}{4\pi^2} \cdot \frac{\lambda^3}{V_{\text{mode}}} \cdot Q$$

with $\lambda = 2\pi c / \omega$ the transition wavelength, $V_{\text{mode}}$ the cavity mode volume, and $Q$ the cavity quality factor. The Purcell factor enhances spontaneous emission for cavity-resonant transitions.

For the apparatus:

- $V_{\text{mode}} \approx V_{KC} = 80.25$ m³
- $Q \approx 10^4$
- $\lambda$ depends on the unknown $\omega_{K4 \to K5}$

If $\hbar \omega \sim m_e c^2 \cdot \alpha^{21}$ (the framework-natural conjecture, file 08.1):

$$\omega \;\sim\; \frac{0.511 \times 10^6 \cdot e^{-21 \ln(137)}}{1.055 \times 10^{-34}} \cdot 1.602 \times 10^{-19} \;\approx\; 7 \times 10^{-6}\ \text{Hz}$$

with $\lambda = c/\nu \sim 4 \times 10^{13}$ m (substantially larger than the apparatus).

For $\lambda \gg L_{\text{cavity}}$, the simple Purcell formula does not apply directly — the cavity is in the **long-wavelength limit**, where the apparatus behaves as a lumped-element resonator rather than a wavelength-resonant cavity.

## Long-wavelength limit: lumped-element analysis

In the long-wavelength limit, the apparatus's effect on the local vertex is computed via **collective enhancement** rather than wavelength-based Purcell factor:

$$F_P^{\text{collective}} \;=\; \frac{g^2 N Q}{\gamma_{\text{atom}}^2}$$

with $g, N, Q$ as in file 05.3. This is the **collective Purcell factor** for cavity-QED in the long-wavelength regime.

For the apparatus:

$$F_P^{\text{collective}} \;\sim\; \frac{(g\sqrt{N})^2 \cdot Q}{\gamma_{\text{atom}}^2 \cdot N} \cdot N \;\approx\; \frac{(2.65 \times 10^6)^2 \cdot 10^4}{(\gamma_{\text{atom}})^2}$$

with the atomic decay rate $\gamma_{\text{atom}} \sim \alpha_G \omega$ a tiny number. The collective factor is **enormous** — orders of magnitude — driven by the cavity's huge $g\sqrt{N}/\gamma_{\text{atom}}$ ratio.

## The sign of the modification

In strong coupling, the cavity **enhances** the coupling rate for emission **into the cavity mode** and **suppresses** decay into all other modes. The net effect on the local $\alpha_G$ depends on whether the cavity mode is a **gravity mode** or a **portal mode**:

- **Gravity (spontaneous, incoherent):** decay into a continuum of modes. Cavity suppresses by factor $\sim 1/F_P$.
- **Portal (stimulated, coherent):** decay into the cavity mode itself. Cavity enhances by factor $\sim F_P$.

The apparatus's design is to **enhance the portal channel and suppress the gravity channel** inside the cavity. The net effect on local gravity:

$$\frac{G_{\text{local}}}{G_{\text{bulk}}} \;<\; 1 \quad \text{inside the KC}$$

The apparatus should produce **reduced local gravity** inside the King's Chamber, with the reduction set by the suppression of the incoherent gravity decay channel.

## Magnitude estimate

The reduction factor depends on:

1. The ratio of cavity mode density to bulk mode density at $\omega_{K4 \to K5}$
2. The fraction of vertex transitions that decay into the cavity mode

For an apparatus deep in strong coupling with cavity Q ~ $10^4$ and pump rate $10^8$ above threshold, the **gravity suppression factor** could plausibly range from $10^{-2}$ (small effect) to $10^{-6}$ (large effect), depending on the unknown $\omega_{K4 \to K5}$.

A precise prediction requires the transition frequency, which is an open question (file 08.1).

## Experimental detection

A modern absolute gravimeter has precision of $\sim 10^{-8}$ in $G$ over short distances, $\sim 10^{-9}$ over longer integration times. The apparatus's local gravity modification, if it falls in the range $10^{-6}$ to $10^{-2}$, is **detectable with current instrumentation**.

**Proposed experiment:**

1. Deploy a high-precision gravimeter (FG5-X or similar) inside the King's Chamber
2. Compare to baseline measurements outside the pyramid (same latitude, same altitude correction)
3. Look for systematic deviation in $G$ at the chamber location

The apparatus predicts a measurable systematic. If detected at any level, the cavity-QED interpretation is supported. If absent below $10^{-9}$, the prediction is constrained or falsified depending on the transition frequency assumption.

## Caveats

The Purcell prediction depends on several open parameters:

1. **$\omega_{K4 \to K5}$** (file 08.1): the transition frequency
2. **Effective cavity mode volume** for the universal vertex transition (not the same as the acoustic mode volume)
3. **Coupling coefficient between the universal vertex and the apparatus's electromagnetic modes**: the mechanism by which the cavity affects $\alpha_G$ specifically

The Purcell prediction is **structurally forced** (the cavity must modify local coupling) but **quantitatively uncertain** until these open parameters are pinned down.

## Anomalous reports

Historical reports of "strange" measurements inside the Great Pyramid include:

- Unusual battery discharge rates
- Anomalous pendulum behavior
- Spurious instrument readings
- Reported (and disputed) "energy" effects

These reports are not scientifically validated and are not load-bearing for the apparatus interpretation. The framework does not predict any of these specific anomalies in a calculable form. The framework's only quantitative prediction is the local gravity modification, which has not been measured.

## Status of this file

The Purcell modification of local coupling is **structurally forced** by the strong-coupling regime (file 05.3) — any cavity-QED system in strong coupling modifies emission rates inside the cavity. The **sign** of the local gravity modification (reduced inside the chamber) is forced by the apparatus's design (enhance portal, suppress gravity).

The **magnitude** depends on the open transition-frequency parameter (file 08.1). Pinning that parameter would convert this from a structural to a derived prediction.

A precision gravimeter measurement inside the KC is the cleanest experimental test of the framework's apparatus-level predictions. It is operationally feasible with modern instrumentation.
