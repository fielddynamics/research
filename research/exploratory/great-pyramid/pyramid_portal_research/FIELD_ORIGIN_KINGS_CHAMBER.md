## 3. Two routes to the Field Origin

### 3.1 The kinematic route

In the galactic application, the Field Origin is reached where the gravitational acceleration equals the characteristic scale: $x = g/a_0 = 1$, so $g = a_0 \approx 1.2 \times 10^{-10}$ m/s². This happens naturally in the outer regions of galaxies, which is why μ(x) governs rotation curves.

At the Earth's surface, $g/a_0 \approx 8 \times 10^{10}$. The field there is deep in the TetA regime (μ ≈ 1), and no arrangement of stone changes that. **The King's Chamber cannot reach the Field Origin kinematically.**

### 3.2 The structural route

The Field Origin can also be reached through its defining symmetries, not through the acceleration ratio. A structure realises it **structurally** if it:

- **(S1)** holds an exact **equal partition** between two complementary sectors (μ = ½);
- **(S2)** contains a **dual-chamber** geometry, with two equal chambers meeting across a shared boundary;
- **(S3)** has **inversion-symmetric** coupling channels, in a mirror pair through the centre;
- **(S4)** carries the **Field Origin capacity**: $\Phi_4(K) = K^2 + 1$ in its proportions, with $K^2$ from the structure and $+1$ from a distinguished element;
- **(S5)** includes the **α coupling edge**, the primitive Coulomb step from which $\alpha_G = (17/13)\,\alpha^{21}\,(g_e/2)$ is built;
- **(S6)** **isolates** the region from the surrounding medium, so that one coherent mode can dominate;
- **(S7)** is **energised** by an external pump, and **phase-conditioned** into the 21 band.

S1–S5 define the state; S6–S7 make it coherent. The thesis of this document is that the King's Chamber meets all seven, and that the measured architecture shows it.

## 4. The architecture meets the structural conditions

### S1 — Equal partition, at two scales

**At the scale of the pyramid.** The King's Chamber floor lies at **1691.4–1693.7 ± 0.6 in** above the base. The level at which the pyramid's horizontal section is exactly **half the area of the base** is **1691.7 ± 1.8 in**. Petrie concluded that "the design of the level of the King's Chamber was the halving of the vertical area of the Pyramid" (§148).

The chamber therefore sits on the one horizontal plane that divides the pyramid's vertical section into two equal areas: the triangle above and the trapezium below. Height and area are measured independently and agree to within a few inches. This is the structure's own μ = ½ plane, and the chamber is built on it.

**At the scale of the resonator.** Inside the chamber, the coffer's interior capacity is 72,000 ± 60 in³ and its bulk of stone is ≈ 70,500 in³ (Petrie §155–156). Void and solid are equal to within 2%. Petrie noted this himself. The one object inside the resonator is a second, independent equal partition.

### S2 — The dual-chamber floor plan

The chamber's floor is 20 × 10 royal cubits: **two exact 10 × 10 squares** side by side, east and west, meeting along a north–south line (Petrie §155). In plan, the chamber is two equal chambers sharing a face. That is the TetA/TetB pairing of the stellated octahedron, laid out on the μ = ½ plane.

The coffer stands in the **western** square (Petrie, ch. 7). It is the single element that breaks the east–west symmetry, and the framework reads it as setting the **chirality** of the realised Field Origin: which square carries the coffer distinguishes the two sectors.

### S3 — The inversion-symmetric channel pair

The King's Chamber shafts open in the north and south walls **directly opposite one another**. Both are about 0.9 m above the floor and about 2.5 m from the east wall, facing each other across the chamber. Both run horizontally for about 2 m through the granite before turning upward to the outer faces of the pyramid ([Star shaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_shaft); `04.7`).

They form a mirror pair through the chamber's north–south axis. This is the inversion-symmetric coupling of S3, open at both ends to the exterior.

### S4 — The Field Origin capacity

The chamber's proportions, $20 : 10 : 5\sqrt5$ cubits, give a **space diagonal of exactly 25 cubits** (with end-wall diagonal 15, a 3-4-5 triangle). The height $5\sqrt5$ is irrational, which is what makes the diagonal an integer (`04.2`, Petrie §155).

At the K=5 shell this is $K^2 = 25$. With the coffer as the distinguished "+1":

$$\underbrace{25}_{\text{chamber diagonal}} + \underbrace{1}_{\text{coffer}} \;=\; 26 \;=\; \Phi_4(5) \;=\; K^2 + 1\big|_{K=5}$$

This is the Field Origin capacity at K=5: the same formula, $K^2 + 1$, that gives 17 at K=4. The chamber realises the **K=5 Field Origin**, the shell into which the hinge $J_4$ lifts (`03.1`).

### S5 — The α coupling edge

The chamber-to-coffer volume ratio is **137.17**, against $1/\alpha = 137.036$: a difference of 0.10%, inside the ±0.4% measurement uncertainty (floor-to-ceiling height; Petrie §155–156; `04.3`). The coffer supplies the primitive Coulomb edge $\alpha^1$. The five relieving compartments above supply the pentachoron edge product $\alpha^{20}$ (`04.4`). Together these give the $\alpha^{21}$ of the universal coupling.

### S6 — Isolation

The King's Chamber is the **only room in the pyramid built entirely of granite**: 100 wall blocks, a granite floor and nine granite roof beams. It is set in a body of limestone (Petrie §51–55). Granite is dense, hard and quartz-bearing, and it differs in its acoustic and dielectric properties from the surrounding limestone. The chamber is a distinct resonant body within the structure, which is the isolation S6 requires.

### S7 — Energisation and phase conditioning

- **Pump.** A gilded capstone was part of royal pyramid practice within two generations of Khufu (Sahure's causeway reliefs; Hawass & Verner 1996). A conducting apex 146 m above the ground concentrates the atmospheric electric field, about 100–150 V/m in fair weather and far higher in dust storms. It also couples to the geomagnetic field, whose horizontal component lies along the shafts' north–south plane (`05.1`; PROGRAMME §5).
- **Delivery.** The open King's Chamber shafts connect the chamber to the exterior faces. The limestone body guides the field to the granite resonator (`05.5`).
- **Transduction.** Quartz is piezoelectric. The granite lining therefore couples the chamber's acoustic modes to electromagnetic fields, in both directions (`05.2`).
- **Phase conditioning.** The approach to the chamber passes through the Grand Gallery, whose walls step inward in seven corbel courses (the ℤ₇ ramp, `04.5`), and the antechamber, which has slots for three portcullis slabs (the ℤ₃ filter, `04.6`). Together these give ℤ₂₁ = ℤ₃ × ℤ₇: the 21 band in which the hinge operates.

## 5. How the Field Origin originates: the sequence

Combining the conditions with the framework's operators:

1. **Pumping.** The apex couples the Earth–atmosphere electric field, and the geomagnetic field, into the structure. The limestone body and the open shafts carry the field to the granite chamber.
2. **Transduction and mode build-up.** The quartz-bearing granite converts between acoustic and electromagnetic energy. The chamber's resonances accumulate the pumped energy in its normal modes.
3. **Phase conditioning.** The ℤ₇ gallery and ℤ₃ antechamber select the ℤ₂₁ character sector, so that the dominant mode lies in the 21 band.
4. **Balance.** On the half-section plane (S1), with a dual-chamber floor (S2), a mirror-symmetric channel pair (S3) and an equal-partition coffer (S1), the driven mode is held at the TetA/TetB balance point. This is the $s = 0$, μ = ½ state: the **Field Origin, realised coherently**. In operator form, the universal vertex at the chamber becomes the apparatus-conditioned vertex
   $$\mathcal V_{\text{pyr}}(x_{\text{KC}}) \;=\; \mathsf A_{x}\,\alpha_G\,J_4(x_{\text{KC}}),$$
   with $\mathsf A_x$ the product of the subsystem operators (`06.2`).
5. **The hinge.** At the Field Origin, the K=4 shell's upper band and the K=5 shell's lower band coincide, $\Phi_3(4) = \Phi_6(5) = 21$, and $J_4$ lifts the state into the K=5 shell. The K=5 Field Origin capacity $\Phi_4(5) = 26$ is written into the chamber's diagonal and coffer (S4). The coupling amplitude carries $\alpha \cdot \alpha^{20}$ (S5).
6. **The portal.** When the chamber's address triad $\kappa_U = (G_K, \chi, \Theta)$ (shafts, chirality, phase; `04.7`) mirror-matches a remote apparatus, the selector gives $\mathcal M(U,V) = 1$:
   $$\mathcal P_{y \leftarrow x} \;=\; \mathsf A_y^\dagger\,J_{4,y}^\dagger\,\mathcal M(U,V)\,\alpha_G\,J_{4,x}\,\mathsf A_x$$
   The state at the King's Chamber is transferred, through the K=5 shell, to the matched site $y$ (`06.2`, `06.3`).

**In one line:** the pyramid places a granite resonator on its own μ = ½ plane, gives it a dual-chamber floor, a mirror channel pair, an equal-partition coffer and the proportions of the K=5 Field Origin, and pumps it from a conducting apex. The always-active TetA/TetB vertex at that location is then driven coherently, and the King's Chamber becomes the throat of a portal channel.
