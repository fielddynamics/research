# The Three Scales — Ancient Japan

Exploratory work. A comparative structural study of Japanese motifs, above all the *mitsuuroko* (三つ鱗, "three scales") crest of the Hōjō regents of the Kamakura shogunate, set beside the face structure of the GFD stellated octahedron: three corner channels around one inverted central core.

The decisive test is depth. At the first level, the Sierpinski triangle (centre removed, 3ⁿ cells) and the GFD subdivision (centre kept as the coupling channel, 4ⁿ cells) look identical, and the figure appears in unrelated traditions, such as the floor of Anagni Cathedral (c. 1104). The programme therefore asks whether any Japanese motif subdivides the centre as well.
