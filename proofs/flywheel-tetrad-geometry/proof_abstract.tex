The four consistently oriented face cycles of the complete four-leg graph and the normalized column-read covectors of the existing row-closed Triforce flywheel independently yield the Gram matrix $4I-J$. We exhibit the partial isometry between them, with its orientation twist, and identify the three-dimensional even circulation sector of the actual twelve-edge octahedral core with the abstract cycle space. An exact current identity links the outer rings to the core without additional dynamical variables. The shared Gram reconstructs the two tetrahedra, eight stellations and octahedral overlap. The result is conditional on the inherited incidence and norm; it does not prove spontaneous formation of an embedded geometry, or a gravitational normalization.
