# Proofs

*Formal results of Gravity Field Dynamics, each with a plain-language summary and a script that checks every step*

---

Each folder holds one proof: the LaTeX source and PDF, a README that explains in prose what the proof says and why it matters, and a verification script that prints PASS or FAIL for every algebraic step.

| Proof | Result | Checks |
|---|---|---|
| [`forced-tetrahedral-frame/`](forced-tetrahedral-frame/) | The legs of a Lorentz tetrad, drawn in three-dimensional space with the pattern of $\eta$ (every pair alike, every leg of equal magnitude) and distinct, are forced to a regular tetrahedron, unique up to $O(3)$. Closure is derived, three is the only dimension in which the shape is forced, the one reading made (by magnitude) is shown against its alternative, and parity completion gives the stellated octahedron | 27/27 |
| [`torsion-regular-representation/`](torsion-regular-representation/) | The 24 torsion components of the tetrad connection form the regular representation of $S_4$, generated by one explicit tensor. The sign representation occurs once in the torsion and never in the Christoffel sector; read by magnitude, the vector and axial parts sit in the incident and non-incident halves; only $K = 2$ and $K = 4$ legs allow the structure | 36/36 |
| [`constitutive-law-exchange-symmetry/`](constitutive-law-exchange-symmetry/) | Under exchange closure of TetA and TetB, the endpoint $\mu(0) = 0$ and coupling of degree one in each variable, the constitutive law is uniquely $\mu(x) = x/(x+1)$. The inversion $x \to 1/x$ about $a_0$ is derived, and each premise is shown to be necessary | 34/34 |
