# Internal mathematical review — Recursive Triforce Closure

This is an adversarial review of the manuscript, not external peer review. The purpose is to state what would falsify or narrow each result rather than to defend the model by adjusting premises after the calculation.

## 1. Does the first subdivision force every later subdivision?

No. The complementary chamber distinguishes the central region on the original face. The four affine maps give a valid recursive partition, not a physical subdivision law. The paper proves the images and their covering with disjoint relative interiors. Closed cells meet on boundaries, and the earlier half-space theorem uses a different boundary ownership convention. Neither choice changes the area or radial measure.

## 2. Is the lattice selected by the tetrad?

The additive span of the twelve octahedral edge midpoints is canonical given that generating set. It is not the only symmetry-invariant lattice compatible with the tetrahedron. Its half-scale refinement is an explicit counterexample to an unconditional stopping rule; the original face is already compatible, so mere compatibility also does not force maximal refinement. The new saturation theorem proves all lattice points on a face are precisely the fifteen quarter-grid points, and the elementary nearest-neighbour mesh is therefore fixed once this lattice and edge rule are used. Physical lattice selection and physical excitation are still open.

## 3. Are there exactly 64 physical amplitudes?

No. There are 64 distinct triangles on each chamber, and central inversion pairs their addresses. The two surfaces can have independent values, and the H36 Hamiltonian does not have 64 resolved secondary amplitudes. The proof concerns the geometry of addresses and the vector space of arbitrary values on that quotient, not the dimension of the physical state space or number of propagating modes.

## 4. Is triangle pointing torsion or chirality?

No. The central similarity is a negative scalar in two dimensions, with positive determinant. The 40/24 split is pointing parity. The traces 21/17/13 are obtained from a specified signed trace functional, not derived source strengths, energy coefficients, or attenuation.

## 5. Does the connection map remove the physical bridge?

No. The explicit A4 intertwiner is a genuine algebraic advance, but it acts on the magnitude-reading permutation representation. It does not identify an affine connection as a derivative of a frame, supply the metric, restore Lorentz covariance, or derive equations of motion. Odd permutations give a trace obstruction for the ordinary pointing-graded action. The modified signed action is an exact algebraic repair, not a measured or derived reflection law. Even within A4 the right-translation counterexample proves nonuniqueness. A single chosen vortex can reduce the symmetry further than A4; no such state has been shown to realize the dictionary. A physical law, not the numerical identity, must select the mapping or establish gauge equivalence among alternatives.

## 6. Does the 21-reader tree derive the pi walk or gravity?

No. The inverse transform reconstructs an arbitrary simultaneous table, conditional on access to its entries. The full FWHT and tree differ by a detail mixer. Burnside counts the invariant subspace under the physical three-cycle; the corner-pair action has different fixed-point counts. The signed pointing operator does not preserve the mean-zero subspace, and its compression supplies a concrete counterexample to the old grading. A collective determinant is a further observable; an orthogonal transform cannot create physical attenuation.

## 7. Does equal area justify equal flux?

No. The radial Jacobian varies over a face. Use its positive solid-angle measure, then show additivity and weighted projection properties. Two enclosing chamber boundaries register the same source twice if added without an ownership rule. No source dynamics follow from the quadrature.
