# Revision 2 — reader and referee review

Basis: the original Proof 26 candidate ZIP, extracted without altering its archived contents. This revision retains the thirteen numbered theorems, the previous proof inventory, source snapshots, figures, interactive explorers and inherited check logs.

## Changes

1. Rewrote the abstract, introduction, section transitions and conclusion around the concrete question of mutually dependent closure. Defined read, settlement and registration before using them.
2. Removed the circular phrasing of premise P1 (a sufficient read was previously assumed). Distinguished feasible selection from a prescribed unique state and set-theoretic factorization from smooth or causal realization. Made the C² hypothesis explicit.
3. Clarified the normalized constant mode (A/8), signed trace formula, norm-row relabeling and the distinction between physical and abstract parity. Retained the inherited P23 obstructions.
4. Added two propositions: basis invariance and all-directions blocking do not uniquely force a determinant; analytic independent blocked-direction identities force a product factor and a minimum possible order of 21. These sharpen the physical source-selection problem.
5. Restored the envelope-force relation from the fixed-geometry response research. Distinguished a susceptibility from a force and fixed the physical potential units in the scalar Green model. Explicitly stated the common-shift hypothesis of recursive registration.
6. Expanded the internal referee guide and identified the decisive microscopic source test.

## Validation

The expanded exact verifier passed 532 assertions, up from 492. The 40 new assertions cover normalized trace versus determinant, orthogonal basis invariance, a counterexample satisfying blocking, 21 separate blocked directions in a polynomial witness, leading-order scaling and the common mode. General analytic statements are proved in prose; tests are not substitutes for those proofs. The earlier 1,352 inherited checks are retained as revision-1 results, not rerun. No new Lean certificate, external referee review, empirical validation or browser test is claimed. No earlier numbered proof or repository file was changed.

The proposed value of G remains conditional on elementary electromagnetic gain, source selection, normalization and a physical long-wave action. The revision strengthens the mathematics and the reader's ability to evaluate the candidate, rather than asserting that these open physical steps are solved.
