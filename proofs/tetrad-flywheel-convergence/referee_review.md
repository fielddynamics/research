# Internal referee review — Proof 25

This is an internal adversarial review, not an external peer review or a physical validation.

| Objection | Resolution in the manuscript |
|---|---|
| The four cycles already assume the answer. | Theorem 1 starts with abstract simple triangles and specified edge-sharing; it proves K4, and gives a common-edge fan counterexample if the multiplicity assumption is removed. The physical incidence premise is stated, not inferred from arbitrary vortices. |
| A zero sum does not force regularity. | Theorem 2 requires the symmetric norm, and gives the forced-frame rank argument. Theorem 6 supplies an explicit stretched equal-length counterexample and sharp anisotropy estimates. |
| Equal Gram matrices may be a coincidence. | Theorem 3 proves exact maps for all finite Euclidean frame realizations, uniqueness on their images, both projector identities and path independence. |
| The cycle frame has different reflection behavior. | The signed complementary-edge involution is explicit. The sign twist under odd permutations is retained; no ordinary full-S4 identification of polar and axial representations is asserted. |
| The full Hadamard matrix has rank four; the other frames have rank three. | Only the three spatial rows enter the common Gram. The full Hadamard is the orthogonal completion, not an additional rank-three frame. |
| The determinant equation has infinitely many tetrad solutions. | The two determinant signs are sectors, not individual solutions. The spatial comparison fixes observer/time orientation and requires the stated realization. |
| The geometry is just a coordinate drawing. | Theorem 4 reconstructs the convex hulls, all sixteen half-space cases, the exact core extreme points and all eight stellation regions intrinsically. Theorem 5 derives the core metric from pair incidence. |
| A current mode is not a vortex and a vortex is not a through-flow. | The three-plus-two split is a dimension of a linear current space. Actual Stage VII currents use its established shared phase and couplings; the four abstract cycles are not the 24 ring bonds. No independent core phase or axial drain is claimed. |
| Edge equality is fitted. | Theorem 6 proves the equivalence conditional on a diagonal metric, recovers every edge weight from the Gram, derives sharp deviation bounds with equality cases, and explicitly does not introduce a restoring force. |
| The 64 addresses establish a torsion action. | The final section keeps Proof 23's lattice premise and reflection obstruction, and does not identify counts with physical torsion, 17/13 or Newton's constant. |
| Lean certification has been overstated. | The audit marks the source unbuilt, specifies coverage and inherited dependencies. Python checks are exact assertions, not kernel theorems. |

**Verdict:** The hand derivations give a conditional convergence theorem with a precisely delimited physical scope. No claim of external acceptance, complete formal verification or physical self-assembly is made. The independent geometry-selection problem remains a separate next proof obligation.
