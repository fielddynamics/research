The preceding tetrad, Hadamard and vortex proofs describe the same regular spatial frame through different constructions. We give a common finite-dimensional formulation that makes their relationship explicit. Four simple triangular cycles with the stated pairwise incidence determine the tetrahedral graph. With the inherited Euclidean norms, the spatial frame, the oriented cycle and cut frames, and the row-closed flywheel phase read have the common Gram matrix $G=4I-J$. Their canonical partial isometries compose independently of the chosen route. A signed complementary-edge involution explains the axial versus polar distinction. Intrinsic closure coordinates recover the two bounded simplices, their octahedral intersection and eight stellations. Pair incidence gives the core metric and the face-dual cube, linking the collective current to the outer phase reads. We prove metric rigidity and sharp anisotropy bounds. These are conditional mathematical equivalences, not a derivation of physical geometry formation or gravitational dynamics from a fixed graph.
