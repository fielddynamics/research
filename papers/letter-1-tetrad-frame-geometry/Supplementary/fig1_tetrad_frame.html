Tetrad Frame Geometry

Supplementary Figure

Construction

Four million points sampled uniformly in local frame coordinates, classified by the sign combination under four projections vi from a single orthonormal tetrad. The four bounding planes generate 24 = 16 half-space combinations, of which exactly two are bounded. Their union is a stellated octahedron whose boundary decomposes into 4 × 4 = 16 independent channels.

Projections

Four oriented planes divide local coordinate space:

  • v1 = x + y + z
  • v2 = x - y - z
  • v3 = -x + y - z
  • v4 = -x - y + z

TetA: max(v1, v2, v3, v4) ≤ 1
TetB: max(-v1, -v2, -v3, -v4) ≤ 1
Octahedral core: TetA ∩ TetB

Colour key

  • Blue — TetA (primary bounded region)
  • Gold — Octahedral core (TetA ∩ TetB)
  • Red — TetB (orientation-reversed bounded region)

Resolution

This visualisation confirms the analytically derived structure proved in the paper. Points are sampled uniformly and classified by the sign combination under the four projections above. No mesh construction or surface fitting is used.
