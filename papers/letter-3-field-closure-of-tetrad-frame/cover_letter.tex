\opening{Dear Editor,}

I am pleased to submit the enclosed manuscript,
\textit{Gravity Field Dynamics: Field Closure of the
Tetrad Frame}, for consideration as a Letter in
\textit{Classical and Quantum Gravity}.  This manuscript
is the third and final letter in a companion sequence
with CQG-115428 (\textit{The Coordinate-Space Geometry
of the Tetrad Frame}) and CQG-115447 (\textit{The Other
Half of Gravity: Torsion and the Two-Chamber Structure
of the Tetrad Frame}), both currently under review at
your journal.

The present letter develops the connection-level
consequence of the two-root tetrad structure established
in the companion papers.  It identifies the
24~antisymmetric components removed by the torsion-free
condition as the second root of the tetrad equation,
shows that restoring them yields the full $K^3 = 64$
component affine connection, and derives the
corresponding covariant action from the constitutive
law alone, with no free parameters or free functions.

Supporting materials include a supplementary
covariant-completion note, a proof of the polynomial
identity used in the main text, an interactive rendering
of the spatial partition, and a Python validation script
for the algebraic results.

Because this manuscript depends directly on results
established in CQG-115428 and CQG-115447, the handling
editor may find it efficient to assign the same referee,
who would already be familiar with the notation and
framework developed across the sequence.

The manuscript has not been submitted elsewhere and is
not under consideration by any other journal.

\closing{Yours sincerely,}
