For composite objects (e.g., an asteroid with $10^{33}$ atoms), field closure operates at radial domain boundaries—the transition surfaces between nested field envelopes—where $k=4$ topology must hold regardless of the object's physical asymmetry; this is the only mechanism allowing continuous closure across scale transitions without averaging or requiring spherical symmetry.
