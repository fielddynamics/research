# Field Closure: Logical Chain

## 1. What is Field Closure?

**Field Closure (FC_Σ):** The process by which distributed field parts achieve simultaneous consistency, enforcing that all coupling channels reach a single coherent state with no inconsistencies.

**Why distributed parts?** The Laplacian ∇²Φ compares a point to its neighbors. You cannot compute field value at a point without knowing neighboring points. Wave propagation requires distinct degrees of freedom at different locations, coupled to neighbors. Not a postulate—follows from LIGO observations and the definition of propagation.

## 2. Why k = 4?

### Simplex Theorem
**In d dimensions, minimum points to enclose region = d+1**

For d=3 spatial dimensions: k = 4 (tetrahedron is simplest 3D solid)

Any system achieving local consistency in 3D space needs at least 4 coupling channels to "close" around a region.

- 1 energy constraint (H = 0) - scalar, no direction

**Total: 4 constraints**

Cannot combine because they transform differently under rotations. The split is forced by geometry of space itself.

**Field closure IS the ADM constraints, expressed in canonical language.**

## 3. The Components

### Field Object
**Definition:** Unified field configuration where multiple quantum fields couple into single correlated state.

**Example:** Asteroid = 10³³ atoms = 10³³ coupled quantum fields forming ONE unified configuration

**Key property:** Cannot couple to part of field object. Like a musical chord—change one note, different chord. The relationships define the entity.

**Mass = field count:** More fields in configuration = more massive

### NET Field
**Definition:** Combined gravitational environment at a location from all upstream contributions.

**Example:** Galaxy experiences cluster field + local group field + cosmic web field → ONE NET Field at galaxy's location

**Key property:** Everything upstream already encoded in local flow. What matters is here, now.

### Closure Boundary (Σ)
**Definition:** Geometric locus where field object meets NET Field. Neutral interface where coupling constraint is enforced.

Not owned by either field—it's where they must achieve consistency.

## 10. Paper Usage Guidelines

### When discussing field closure, emphasize:

1. **Relational nature:** Settlement between configuration and ambient field (not "within" object)

2. **Scale independence:** Works identically from electron to galaxy cluster (not "at tile scale")

3. **Continuous operation:** Ongoing process, not one-time (no lag, no wakes)

4. **Unified structure:** Both propagation AND coupling always present (not spatial "within/between")

5. **Empirical constraints:** Lossless and local requirements from observations (not theoretical preferences)

### Red flags to avoid:

- "Field Origin only at tile scale"
- "Settlement within field objects"
- "Between field objects" vs "Within field objects" (spatial dichotomy)
- Suggesting some objects use Field Origin, others don't
- Implying closure is internal rather than relational
