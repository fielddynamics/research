```
Paper IV established that gravitational Field Closure requires k=4 coupling 
channels. Paper V proved that k=4 Coupling Ports produce a tetrahedral Field 
Chamber. This paper addresses whether a single Field Chamber suffices.

It does not. Established physics requires dual structures: spinor structure 
demands a global tetrad field with double cover (Weinberg 1995, textbook; 
Geroch 1968, foundational); gravity's chiral decomposition requires both 
A⁺ and A⁻ (Ashtekar 1986, foundational); and ghost-free bimetric gravity 
requires two dynamical metrics (Hassan-Rosen 2012, foundational). These 
results—with over 2,300 combined citations—establish that gravitational 
field theory requires two tetrahedral structures.

Classical geometry provides the arrangement. The stellated octahedron is 
the unique compound of two tetrahedra (Coxeter 1973, textbook). Their 
intersection forms a regular inner octahedron at the center. The symmetric 
vielbein condition (Deffayet et al. 2013) specifies where two tetrads can 
consistently couple—precisely at this central region.

Everything above is established physics and geometry. This paper's 
contribution is the synthesis and a novel derivation: we identify the 
stellated octahedron as the unique realization of the dual tetrahedral 
requirement, and we derive electron spin from the resulting structure. 
The dual structure has 8 faces: 2 TIME faces (inward) and 6 SPATIAL 
faces (outward). Spin is the TIME-face fraction of total angular momentum:
S = (2/8) × 2ℏ = ℏ/2.

This spin derivation is the paper's novel claim. The structural foundation 
is textbook.
```
