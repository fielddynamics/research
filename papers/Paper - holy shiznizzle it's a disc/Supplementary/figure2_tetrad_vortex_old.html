The Two-Chamber Field Capacity

The geometric volumes of the two tetrad sectors.

■ Upper Funnel (TetA): Extends above by μ(x)

▬ Disk Midplane: The capacity transfer boundary

■ Lower Funnel (TetB): Extends below by 1 - μ(x)

Kinematic construction: The physical capacity of the field is plotted
as spatial extensions from the disk. The total enclosed shape is an
hourglass that pinches exactly at the MOND ring (where both = 0.5).
