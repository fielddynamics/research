## Spine (7 sections)

### Section 1: The temporal face coupling

**Opens with:** The stellated octahedron has 8 faces. Each tetrahedron 
carries a 3+1 decomposition (scheme proof). The temporal face of TetA 
is (+,+,+); of TetB is (−,−,−). These are the ONLY faces with 
uniform sign.

**Key result:** The temporal faces are perfectly antiparallel (dot = −1).
They span a single direction in R³. The coupling between the two 
time directions is head-on, along one axis. The spatial faces span 
the full 3D and have mixed signs.

**Why it matters:** The non-spatial coupling between the two roots 
occurs along the temporal direction. This is where the two chambers 
"meet" — not in space, but in time.

### Section 2: The parity operator and the two-state basis

**Opens with:** Spatial parity P: eⁱ → −eⁱ sends every TetA face 
normal to the corresponding TetB face normal. P² = I (involution).

**Key result:** This defines a natural Z₂ symmetry with two states:
|TetA⟩ and |TetB⟩. On this 2D Hilbert space:
- σ_x (Pauli X) = parity operator (swaps roots)
- σ_z (Pauli Z) = root observable (+1 for TetA, −1 for TetB)
- Both square to I
- σ_x exchanges the eigenstates of σ_z

**Why it matters:** The two roots of the tetrad equation carry the 
same algebraic structure as a spin-½ system. This is not an analogy — 
it's the same group theory (Z₂ symmetry with two irreps).

### Section 3: The projection operators

**Opens with:** The standard quantum projectors onto the two 
eigenstates:
  P₊ = (I + σ_z)/2 → projects onto |TetA⟩
  P₋ = (I − σ_z)/2 → projects onto |TetB⟩

**Key results:**
- P₊² = P₊ (idempotent) ✓
- P₋² = P₋ (idempotent) ✓
- P₊ + P₋ = I (completeness) ✓
- P₊ P₋ = 0 (orthogonality) ✓

**The connection to μ:** Define the state at acceleration x as:
  |ψ(x)⟩ = √μ |TetA⟩ + √(1−μ) |TetB⟩

Then ⟨ψ|P₊|ψ⟩ = μ(x). The constitutive law IS the expectation 
value of the TetA projector.

**Why it matters:** μ = x/(x+1) was derived from exchange symmetry 
(Letter 2). Here it acquires a second identity: it's the Born-rule 
probability of finding the system in the TetA state. The 
idempotency holds at the operator level even though μ is continuous.

**Opens with:** At x = 1 (the MOND ring): s = 0, μ = 1/2.

**The state:** |ψ(1)⟩ = (|TetA⟩ + |TetB⟩)/√2. This is the

**Von Neumann entropy:** S = −μ ln μ − (1−μ) ln(1−μ). At μ = 1/2:

**The interaction strength:** μ(1−μ) = 1/4 at x = 1. This is both 
the peak of the logistic transition rate AND the peak of the

**Why it matters:** The MOND ring isn't just a kinematic crossover.

**Key identity:** μ(1−μ) = (1−s²)/4

This is the standard spin-½ result: the variance of a σ_z

**Consequences:**

- The logistic identity x dμ/dx = μ(1−μ) IS the statement that

- Inner region (x ≫ 1): pure TetA, entropy ≈ 0
